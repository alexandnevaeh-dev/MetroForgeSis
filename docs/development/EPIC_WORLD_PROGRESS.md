The top-down playable set is in `GeneratedGames/test-games/topdown/current`. Launch `Play Top-down Test.cmd` from the repository. Its art revision is 5 and its 3D presentation revision is 3. The separate Stormglass side-view set is in `GeneratedGames/test-games/metroidvania/current`; launch `Play Side-view Test.cmd`. Keep each set's assets, saves, world data and controllers separate.

The Canopy has sixteen connected woodland areas, three factions, readable testimonies, two quest givers and three learnable spells. WASD/arrows move, J/X attacks, Shift dodges, E interacts, Escape pauses. Seedflare (Q) fires an ember projectile, Rootward (R) grants temporary bark armor and Bloomstep (F) extends the collision-safe invulnerable dash. Quests, essence, spell cooldowns and learned abilities persist through the actual save system.

HD-2D presentation uses real 3D terrain, pixel-textured timber houses, roofs, lighting, shadows and restrained fog. Rivers sit 18 units below the walking surface; shore banks extend beneath the water and bridges have decks and piers. The 2D world remains authoritative for collision and controller timing. Visible 3D sprites mirror their actual animation frames with nearest filtering and the same foot anchor. The generic generator supports the presentation through an HD-2D style request; the sixteen-area narrative and spells are still a curated test game.
