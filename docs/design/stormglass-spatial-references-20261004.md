# Stormglass castle spatial references - 2026-10-04

User supplied three reference images: a steampunk clock-tower progression sketch, a stacked castle map, and a large connected castle cutaway. They guide spatial language, not asset copying. The clock-tower sketch shows a central elevator/shaft, horizontal exploration wings, quiet save alcoves, visible ability gates, a hidden lower wall and a shortcut door. The castle cutaways show continuous structural floors, vertically stacked galleries, stairs, towers, windows, and long stretches of inhabited interior.

## Next room redesign

Build a long gallery with several connected architectural bays rather than stretching one small room. Retain the current player/camera scale. Use a lower route, balcony route, and a readable vertical connection where movement permits; make upgrades expose useful return routes and shortcuts rather than simply requiring a longer walk. Save/shrine alcoves and boss arenas retain distinct pacing and silhouettes.

Attach playable shelves to visible corbels, wall piers or gallery spandrels. Ground props on actual complete support surfaces and place bookcases/statues/altars in architectural recesses. Foreground floor edges, wall masonry and platform trim need matching material, scale and lighting. Rear bays and distant lancets recede in contrast and size; foreground arches frame, rather than obscure, the actor. All districts remain parts of one castle, with water, archive damage and frost as condition changes.

The current renderer scales one entire panorama to cover the whole room, so greater room dimensions can enlarge details and reduce coherence. A modular bay renderer and collider-aligned structural art are required before broad width expansion. Changing target sizes alone is insufficient. The current accepted game's 40 rooms and ability routes are preserved pending actual native traversal of replacements.

## Sources and observations

- [Castlevania environments](https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Environments): distinct interior districts within the same castle.
- [Galleries and marble corridors](https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Galleries_and_Marble_Corridors): long corridors, columns, windows and wall decoration.
- [Churches, cathedrals and chapels](https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Churches,_Cathedrals_and_Chapels): vertical towers and stairs, stained glass. Royal Chapel screenshot inspected in the browser: bright foreground stone frames a deep receding nave with smaller repeated windows and light shafts.
- [Metroidvania level design](https://www.flyriver.com/g/metroidvania-levels): interconnected exploration, ability gates, loops, secrets and visual cues.

These room specifications are our application of the references. They are not claims that the current game already implements the new layout. Top-down assets, rooms and animations stay separate. No franchise art is imported into game assets.
